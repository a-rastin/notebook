01 / Assessment context

Age & rating instructions

This interface is for adults aged 18+. DSM-5-TR uses a threshold of 5 symptoms per domain from age 17 onward.

Check a symptom only if it occurs often and has persisted for at least 6 months. Rate difficulties that are inappropriate for developmental level and adversely affect social, academic, or occupational activities. Do not count behavior explained solely by defiance, hostility, or misunderstanding instructions.

Unchecked = not endorsed. After evaluating all nine items in a domain, confirm that you have reviewed the complete list.

Scoring method & references

Each endorsed symptom contributes 1 to its own domain. A domain reaches threshold at 5/9. Counts are not measures of severity or diagnostic probability. The total out of 18 is descriptive only: symptoms cannot be pooled across domains to reach threshold.

Presentation is based on which domains meet threshold during the last 6 months. The overall checklist is consistent with criteria only when both symptom lists are reviewed, adult age is valid, at least one domain reaches threshold, and all clinical requirements are confirmed. Unknown answers remain unresolved. This tool does not assign severity, partial remission, or diagnostic codes, and cannot evaluate an established diagnosis when symptoms are reduced by treatment.
